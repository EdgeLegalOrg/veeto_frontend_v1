Roles

A role is a named set of rights. Every user of a company holds one role, and the role also decides which billing tier the user is charged at.

The list

ColumnMeaning
RoleThe name, with its description underneath.
RightsHow many rights the role grants.
UsersHow many users hold the role.
Billed asThe billing tier: Standard user or Power user.

Search by role name or description.

Adding a role

  1. Open the company's Roles tab and choose Add role.
  2. Type a Role name (required) and, if you like, a Description.
  3. Choose what the role is Billed as.
  4. Tick the Rights the role grants (see below).
  5. Choose Save.

Choosing rights

Rights are grouped by category. The counter above the list reads, for example, 12 of 40 rights selected.

  • Tick a right to grant it. Hover over its name to see its description.
  • Choose toggle all beside a category heading to tick or clear the whole category.

Editing a role

Choose Edit on the role's row, change what you need and choose Save. Changes to rights apply to everyone who holds the role.

Setting the billing tier

You can change the tier straight from the list: use the Billed as menu on the role's row. It saves at once and a message confirms it. The company is charged its rate for that tier for each active user in the role. See How billing works.

Deleting a role

Choose Delete and confirm. A role that still has users cannot be deleted: the button is disabled, and if you try anyway you are told That role is still assigned to users, so it cannot be deleted. Move the users to another role first, on the Users tab.

Every change here is recorded in the audit log, including tier changes.
