Cost records

Generate, check, finalise and download each company's cost for a month.

The Billing page

Choose Billing at the top. Pick a Month (not before 2000 and not in the future). The table lists every company:

ColumnMeaning
CompanyOpens the company's page.
Rate: standard / powerThe company's two rates, with A rate is not set if either is blank.
Standard users, Power usersCounts in the record, or a dash if none has been generated.
TotalThe record's total.
StatusDraft, Finalised or Not generated.

A total for all listed records appears at the foot of the table. Search by company or status.

Generating a record

  • For one company: choose Generate on its row. If a draft already exists the button reads Regenerate.
  • For every company: choose Generate for all companies and confirm.

You can also generate from the company's own Billing tab: pick the month and choose Generate.

What generating for all does

  • Draft records for the month are replaced with a fresh snapshot.
  • Finalised records are left alone.
  • A company with users on a tier whose rate is not set is skipped, and listed afterwards.

When it finishes, a window reports how many were generated, how many were already finalised and how many could not be generated, and why.

A record captures who is registered and unlocked at the moment you generate it, so generate it for the month just ended or the current one.

The record page

Choose View to open a record. It shows its status, who generated it and when, then:

  • The total for the month, and the Standard and Power user counts with the rates used.
  • A table of What each site pays. Choose a site's name to list the users behind its figures, with their role and tier.

The buttons on a record

ButtonWhat it doesWhen
Download CSVSaves the record as a CSV file, named for the company and month.Always
RegenerateReplaces the draft with a fresh snapshot.Draft only
DeleteRemoves the draft. You can generate it again.Draft only
FinaliseKeeps the record exactly as it is.Draft only

A finalised record can no longer be regenerated or deleted. If you try, you are told That cost record has been finalised, so it can no longer be regenerated or deleted. Check the figures before you finalise.

Generating, regenerating, finalising and deleting are all recorded in the audit log.
