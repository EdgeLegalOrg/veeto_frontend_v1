Setting rates and tiers

Set what each company pays per user per month, and put each role on the right tier.

Setting a company's rates

  1. Open the company and choose its Billing tab.
  2. Under Monthly rate per user, type the Standard user and Power user rates in dollars per month.
  3. Choose Save rates. A message confirms Rates saved.

Below the form you can see when the rates were last changed and by whom.

What a rate can be

  • A number from 0 to 100000, with at most two decimal places.
  • Leave a rate blank if it is not set: the field shows Not set.

Anything else is refused with A rate must be a number from 0 to 100000 with at most two decimal places.

If any role is on the Power tier and the Power rate is blank, the tab warns you that a cost record cannot be generated until it is set.

Changing a rate later

Records already generated keep the rate they were generated at. A new rate applies to records you generate or regenerate afterwards.

Putting a role on a tier

  1. Open the company's Roles tab.
  2. In the Billed as column, choose Standard user or Power user for the role.

It saves at once. Every user in that role moves to that tier. See Roles.

New companies

A company created in the portal starts with no rates set. Set them before the first month's record is generated. A role copied from another company when you create a company keeps its tier, but the rates are not copied.

Rate and tier changes are recorded in the audit log.
