The companies list

Every company on the platform, with its size, and the way in to manage any one of them.

What the list shows

ColumnMeaning
CompanyThe company's name, and its number underneath.
LocationCity and state from the company's details.
SitesActive sites. If some are inactive it reads, for example, 2 of 3.
RolesHow many roles the company has.
UsersHow many users have a login at one of its sites.

What you can do

  • Search by name, city, state or company number. See Searching and paging lists.
  • Choose Open, or the company's name, to go to its company page.
  • Choose Create company to set up a new one.

Each company's sites, roles and users are kept apart from every other company's. Nothing you do in one company's page can reach another's.
