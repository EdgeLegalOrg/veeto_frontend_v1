Sites

A site is one office of a company. Matters, staff, numbering and storage are kept per site.

The list

ColumnMeaning
SiteThe name, and its number underneath.
CodeA short code, if one was given.
Time zoneThe office's time zone.
GSTThe GST rate as a percentage, or Not set if the company has not set one.
UsersHow many users can work at the site: those assigned to it, plus everyone whose role covers all sites.
StatusActive or Inactive.

Search by name, code, time zone, number or status.

Adding a site

  1. Open the company's Sites tab and choose Add site.
  2. Fill in the form (below) and choose Save.

Editing a site

Choose Edit on the site's row, change what you need and choose Save.

The fields

  • Site name (required), Site code, ABN, Email, Website.
  • Time zone: start typing and choose from the list (for example Australia/Sydney). If left blank it is set to Australia/Sydney.
  • GST rate (%): from 0 to 100. Blank means the company has to set it before it can invoice.
  • Financial year start: choose a day (DD) and a month (MM) from the lists, as on the company's own Edit Site form. The Financial Year End is worked out for you. Leave both blank for 1 July to 30 June. A day that does not exist in the month (for example 31 in month 02) is refused.
  • Active: an inactive site cannot be newly assigned to anyone. The first site of a company must stay active.

Sites are never deleted. Make a site inactive when an office closes.
