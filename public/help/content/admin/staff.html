Staff

The people who work at a company, with or without a login. A staff member's site is where their cost is billed.

The list

ColumnMeaning
NameTitle, first and last name, with their occupation underneath.
ContactEmail, with phone and mobile underneath.
SiteThe site they are based at.
LoginTheir username (with a Locked badge if locked), or No login.
StatusActive or Inactive.

Filtering and searching

  • The first menu limits the list to one site, or All sites.
  • The second shows Active and inactive, Active only or Inactive only.
  • The search box finds a name, email, phone or login.

See also Searching and paging lists.

Adding a staff member

  1. Open the company's Staff tab and choose Add staff member.
  2. Enter the First name and Last name (required). Title, middle name, emails, phones, mobile, occupation and practising certificate number are optional.
  3. Choose the site in Based at (required). Their cost is billed under this site.
  4. Leave Active ticked, or clear it.
  5. Choose Save.

Editing and making inactive

  • Edit opens the same form for changes.
  • Make inactive / Make active switches the status at once.

Making someone inactive does not lock their login. To stop them signing in, lock the user on the Users tab.

Logins

Logins are created on the Users tab, not here. If a staff member's record cannot be found in the company you are working in, you are told That staff member could not be found in this company.

Why the site matters for billing

A user is billed once, at the site of their staff record. See How billing works.
