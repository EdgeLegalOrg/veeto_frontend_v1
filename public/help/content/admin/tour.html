Under the bar, a yellow banner reminds you that you are signed in as a platform administrator and that what you change affects every company.

The dashboard

The top of the dashboard shows the number of companies, sites, company users and active system admins. Below that is every function a company administrator has in the application's Admin menu, grouped (Company, People and access, Checklists, Documents, Communication, Platform). Where the portal can do the same job for any company, a Manage in this portal link opens it; the others only describe what the company can do in the application.

Things every list has

  • A search box at the top that narrows the list as you type.
  • Pages of 20 rows, with a choice of 20, 50 or 100 and the page buttons underneath.

See Searching and paging lists.

Buttons you will see often

  • Edit opens a form in a window; Cancel closes it without saving.
  • Delete and Remove ask you to confirm. If something is still in use, the portal says so and does not delete it.
  • A green or red message at the top right confirms or explains what happened.
