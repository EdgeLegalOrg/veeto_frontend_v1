Your account and password

Choose Account at the top right to see who you are signed in as and to change your password.

Changing your password

  1. Choose Account.
  2. Type your Current password.
  3. Type your New password, then type it again in Confirm new password.
  4. Choose the button to save.

What a password must be

  • 12 to 64 characters.
  • At least one upper case letter, one lower case letter, one number and one symbol.
  • No spaces.

The new password must also be different from the current one. If it does not meet the rule, or is the same as the current one, you are told that a system admin password needs at least 12 characters, with upper and lower case letters, a number and a symbol.

What happens when you change it

  • You stay signed in on this page.
  • Any other sign-in you had open, on another computer or tab, is ended.
  • The change is recorded in the audit log (the password itself never is). A wrong current password is recorded too.

When you must change it

If your account is new, or another system admin has reset your password, the page is titled Choose a new password and you cannot open any other page until you have done it.

You cannot lock, remove or reset your own account. If you are locked out, another system admin has to help. See System admins.
