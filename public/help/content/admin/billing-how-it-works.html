How billing works

Each company is charged a monthly rate for every registered, unlocked user. The rate depends on the user's tier, and the tier comes from the user's role.

The pieces

PieceWhat it isWhere you set it
TierStandard user or Power user.On each role (Roles)
RateDollars per user per month, one for each tier, set per company.The company's Billing tab (Setting rates and tiers)
Cost recordA snapshot of one company's users and charges for one month.Cost records

Who is counted

  • Users who are registered and not locked at the moment the record is generated.
  • Each user is counted once, under the site of their staff record, even if they can work at other sites.
  • A user's tier is the tier of their role. A user whose role has no tier set is a Standard user. A user with no role is counted as Standard and shown as No role.

A record's total is the number of Standard users times the Standard rate, plus the number of Power users times the Power rate. Each site's line shows what that site pays.

Records are snapshots

  • A record captures the users, roles and rates as they were when it was generated. Changing a user, a role or a rate afterwards does not change it.
  • A draft can be regenerated (a fresh snapshot) or deleted.
  • A finalised record is kept exactly as it is. It can no longer be regenerated or deleted.

A rate must be set first

A record cannot be generated if the company has users on a tier whose rate is not set. You are told Set the rate for every user tier this company has users on before generating a cost record. A tier with no users does not need a rate.

The portal records the cost. It does not issue invoices or take payment.

A typical month

  1. Check each company's rates and that each role is on the right tier.
  2. On the Billing page, choose the month and generate the records.
  3. Open a record, check it, and download the CSV if you need it elsewhere.
  4. Finalise the record to lock it.
