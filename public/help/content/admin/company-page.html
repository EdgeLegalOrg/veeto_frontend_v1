The company page

Everything about one company, in tabs.

Open a company from the companies list. The heading shows the company's name and, underneath, its number and how many sites, roles and users it has.

The Details tab

  1. Open the Details tab.
  2. Change the company's name, address, contact details, DX details or disclaimer. The name is required.
  3. Choose Save. A message confirms Company saved.

The change is recorded in the audit log (COMPANY_UPDATED).

Everything on these tabs belongs to the company in the heading. You cannot reach another company's sites, roles, users or settings from here, even by changing an address.
