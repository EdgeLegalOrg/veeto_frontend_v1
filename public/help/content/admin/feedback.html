Feedback review

What users have said about the application, from every company, with the company shown on each response.

Finding feedback

  1. Choose a Company, or leave it on All companies.
  2. Choose a Site of that company, or All sites. (The site menu needs a company first. Changing the company clears the site.)
  3. Use the search box to find a person, office or role.

The columns

ColumnMeaning
CompanyThe company the response came from.
PersonThe person's name, with their username underneath. Choose it to open the response.
Signed in atThe site they were signed in at.
Answered forThe office the answers were given for, with the role underneath.
SubmittedWhen they submitted.

Reading a response

Choose the person's name to open it. Each question is shown with the answer:

  • A Rating, if the question asked for one.
  • The written answer, if there is one.
  • Not applicable, if the person marked it so.
  • Any Comment they added.

If nothing was recorded you see No answers were recorded.

Feedback is read-only in the portal. It contains what people wrote about their own work, so share it only with those who need it.
