System admins

The people who can sign in to this portal. They belong to no company.

The list

ColumnMeaning
UsernameTheir username, with (you) against your own and their email underneath.
NameTheir display name.
StatusActive or Locked, plus Password change pending if they must choose a new password.
Last sign-inWhen they last signed in.

Adding a system admin

  1. Choose Add system admin.
  2. Type a Username: 3 to 50 letters, numbers or the symbols . _ @ -. It cannot be changed later.
  3. Type their Name and, if you like, an Email.
  4. Optionally type a Temporary password that meets the admin password rule. Leave it blank to have one generated.
  5. Choose Save, then copy the temporary password from the window and give it to them. It is shown only once.

They must change the password at their first sign-in. See Temporary passwords.

What you can do to an admin

ButtonWhat it does
EditChange their name or email.
Reset passwordGives them a new temporary password. They are signed out and must choose their own at next sign-in.
LockSigns them out immediately. They cannot sign in until unlocked.
UnlockLets them sign in again.
RemoveRemoves them permanently. Their past actions stay in the audit log.

Safeguards

  • You cannot lock, remove or reset your own account. Use Account to change your own password. If you try you are told You cannot do that to your own account. Ask another system admin.
  • The last active system admin can never be locked or removed, so someone can always sign in. You are told That would leave no active system admin, so nobody could sign in to the portal.
  • A username that is already taken is refused: That username is already taken.

Five wrong passwords in a row lock an account for 15 minutes on their own. See Signing in. Another system admin can use Lock and Unlock, or Reset password, to help someone back in.

All of these actions are recorded in the audit log.
