About the System Admin Portal

The System Admin Portal is where the people who run Veeto set up and look after every company on it.

Who it is for

It is for system admins: platform operators who belong to no company. A company's own administrators use the Admin menu inside the application. The portal can do the same jobs for any company, and some that the application's Admin menu cannot (creating a company, billing, the shared list of matter types).

What it does not do

  • It does not show a company's matters, contacts, documents, invoices or trust accounts.
  • It does not bill a company. It produces the cost record; sending the bill is a separate step.
  • It does not replace a company's own Admin menu. Both change the same information.

Things to keep in mind

What you change here affects real companies. A banner at the top of every page says so. Matter types are shared by every company at once. Everything you change is recorded in the audit log with your name.

You sign in separately from the application: your portal account does not open the application and an application account does not open the portal. See Signing in.

If the sign-in page says The system admin portal is not enabled on this server, the server has not been given its portal signing secret yet. Ask whoever looks after the server. See Troubleshooting.
