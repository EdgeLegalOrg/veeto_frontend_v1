Signing in

The portal has its own sign-in, separate from the one people use to open Veeto.

To sign in

  1. Open the address of Veeto followed by /admin-portal.
  2. Type your Username and Password.
  3. Choose Sign in.

Your first sign-in

A new account, or one whose password has just been reset, is given a temporary password. When you sign in with it you are taken straight to Choose a new password and cannot use anything else until you have. See Your account and password.

If you get the password wrong

  • The page says Invalid username or password. It says the same whether the username or the password was wrong, so nobody can use it to find out which usernames exist.
  • After 5 wrong passwords in a row the account is locked for 15 minutes (these are the standard settings; whoever looks after the server can change them).
  • Once the account is locked, the page says This account is locked if you then give the right password. Wait, or ask another system admin to unlock or reset it.

How long you stay signed in

A sign-in lasts 2 hours as standard. After that, or if you close the browser tab, you are returned to the sign-in page. You are also signed out if another system admin locks your account, resets your password, or you change your password in another window.

Signing out

Choose Sign out at the top right.

The portal and the application never share a sign-in. Signing in to the portal does not sign you in to Veeto, and a Veeto user cannot open the portal by any link or address.
