Users

A user is a login to Veeto. Use this tab to add a login, change someone's role and sites, lock or unlock them, and give them a new temporary password.

The list

ColumnMeaning
UserThe username, with the person's name underneath.
RoleTheir role, or a No role warning if they have none.
SitesThe sites they are assigned to, or All sites if their role covers every site.
StatusActive, or Locked.

Search by username, name, role or site.

Adding a user

  1. Open the company's Users tab and choose Add user. (It is unavailable until the company has at least one role.)
  2. Enter a Username, First name and Last name (all required), and an Email if you have one.
  3. Choose the Role.
  4. Tick the Sites the user works at. At least one is needed unless the role covers every site.
  5. Optionally type a Temporary password: 8 to 20 characters. Leave it blank and one is generated.
  6. Choose Save.
  7. A window shows the temporary password. Copy it and give it to the user. It is shown only once.

The user must choose their own password the first time they sign in. See Temporary passwords.

Changing a role or sites

  1. Choose Role and sites on the user's row.
  2. Choose a different Role, and tick or clear sites.
  3. Choose Save.

If the role covers every site, you do not need to tick any: the user can work at every active site. Tick sites only to record where the person is based.

A user's billing tier comes from their role, so changing the role can change what the company is charged. See How billing works.

Locking and unlocking

Choose Lock to stop someone signing in, or Unlock to let them back. It takes effect at once and is recorded in the audit log. The user's role, sites and staff record are left as they are.

Resetting a password

  1. Choose Reset password on the user's row.
  2. Confirm. Their old password stops working.
  3. Copy the new temporary password from the window and pass it on.

They must choose their own password at their next sign-in.

Where staff come in

A person can be a staff member without a login. A login is created here, on the Users tab, not on the Staff tab. Making a staff member inactive does not lock their login.
