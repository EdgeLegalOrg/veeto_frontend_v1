Logged in users

Who is signed in to the application, and the history of sign-ins, across every company.

Filtering

  1. Choose a Company, or leave it on All companies.
  2. Choose a Site of that company, or All sites. The site menu is available only once a company is chosen, and shows that company's sites (inactive ones are marked).
  3. Use the search box to find a user, site or address.
  4. Choose one of the three tabs:
    • Signed in now: sessions that are still open.
    • Signed out: sessions that have ended.
    • All: both.

Changing the company clears the site. Choose Refresh to reload the list.

The columns

ColumnMeaning
CompanyThe company the user belongs to.
UserThe person's name, with their username underneath.
SiteThe site they signed in at.
Signed inWhen they signed in.
LengthHow long the session lasted, or has lasted so far if it is still open. Shown in minutes, or hours and minutes.
StatusSigned in, or why it ended, with the time it ended.
FromThe network address the sign-in came from.

Why a session ended

Shown asMeaning
Signed outThe user signed out.
Timed outThe session timed out.
ExpiredThe login could no longer be valid, so the session was closed automatically.
Forced outThe user was forced out.

A session is closed when someone signs out, times out or is forced out. One that never reported, for example because the browser was closed, is closed automatically once its login could no longer be valid. It may therefore show as signed in for a while after the person has gone.

This page is read-only. To stop a person signing in, lock the user on the company's Users tab.
