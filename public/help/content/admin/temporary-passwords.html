Temporary passwords

When the portal creates a login or resets a password it gives you a temporary password to pass on. It is shown once.

When you get one

  • Creating a company (the first administrator).
  • Adding a company user, or resetting their password.
  • Adding a system admin, or resetting theirs.

The window

A window shows the password with a Copy button. It says the password is shown once and cannot be retrieved again.

  1. Choose Copy.
  2. Give it to the person through a channel you trust.
  3. Close the window.

Once you close the window the password cannot be shown again. If it is lost, reset the password and a new one is shown.

Choosing your own, or generating one

The form asks for a temporary password as an optional field. Leave it blank and one is generated. If you type one, it must meet these rules:

ForRule
Company users and a company's first administrator8 to 20 characters.
System admins12 to 64 characters, with at least one upper case letter, one lower case letter, one number and one symbol, and no spaces.

What happens next

  • The person must choose their own password the first time they sign in. Until they do, the temporary one is all they can use.
  • A reset ends the old password straight away. A system admin who is reset is also signed out everywhere.
  • In the system admins list, Password change pending shows who has not yet chosen their own.

The password is never written to the audit log. The log records only that a user was created or a password was reset.
